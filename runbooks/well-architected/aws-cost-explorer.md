# AWS Cost Explorer

[AWS Cost Explorer](https://aws.amazon.com/aws-cost-management/aws-cost-explorer/)  lets you visualize, understand, and manage your AWS costs and usage over time. You can analyze your cost and usage data at a high level (for example, total costs and usage across all accounts in your organization) or for highly specific requests (for example, `m2.2xlarge` costs within account Y that are tagged project: secretProject).

[

#### Getting Started

](#getting-started)

First you need to [Enable Cost Explorer](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/awsaccountbilling/latest/aboutv2/ce-enable.html)  in order to use it.

Open [Cost Explorer](https://console.aws.amazon.com/billing/home?/costexplorer#/costexplorer)  and go to "Recommendation tab".

Review recommended actions and potential savings provided by the builtin checks.

![Cost Explorer Recommendations](https://static.us-east-1.prod.workshops.aws/public/8844589c-f6be-4752-bd27-0dad323d1eb9/static/images/003_tools/005_cost_explorer/recommendations.png?classes=shadow&width=1024px)

You'll get more details in the blog post [Launch: Resource Optimization Recommendations](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws-cost-management/launch-resource-optimization-recommendations/) .

Here is a list of actions you can take to fully explore the benefits of AWS Cost Explorer:

*   Tip #1: Aggregate Cost per service
*   Tip #2: Aggregate Costs per project or costs centers
*   Tip #3: Identify unexpected trends
*   Tip #4: Explore Auto-Generated Trend Reports to understand you AWS usage
*   Tip #5: Generate a Compute Purchase Type Report to understand your Spot; Savings Plans; On-Demand usage
*   Tip #6: Explore recommendations
*   Tip #7: Observe forecasting - [Forecasting with Cost Explorer](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/awsaccountbilling/latest/aboutv2/ce-forecast.html) 
*   Tip #8: Analyze data transfer costs - [Using AWS Cost Explorer to analyze data transfer costs](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/mt/using-aws-cost-explorer-to-analyze-data-transfer-costs/)
