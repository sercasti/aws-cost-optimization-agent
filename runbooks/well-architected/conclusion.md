##### AWS Well-Architected Framework

](#aws-well-architected-framework)

Cost optimization is one of the five [AWS Well-Architected Framework](https://aws.amazon.com/architecture/well-architected)  pillars.

The AWS Well-Architected Framework helps you build secure, high-performing, resilient, and efficient infrastructure. The five pillars are:

*   Operational excellence,
*   Security,
*   Reliability,
*   Performance efficiency, and
*   Cost optimization

The AWS Well-Architected provides a consistent approach to evaluate architectures and implement designs that can scale over time.

AWS provides whitepapers and questions that you can apply to your infrastructure.

Doing so will help you foster your cloud excellence.

You can conduct a Well-Architected review yourself from the [AWS Well-Architected Tool](https://console.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/home) . You can also ask for support from you [AWS account team](https://aws.amazon.com/contact-us/) .
