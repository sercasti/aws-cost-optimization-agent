# AWS Trusted Advisor

[AWS Trusted Advisor](https://aws.amazon.com/premiumsupport/technology/trusted-advisor/)  is an online tool that provides you real time guidance to help you provision your resources following AWS best practices. Trusted Advisor checks help optimize your AWS infrastructure, increase security and performance, reduce your overall costs, and monitor service limits. Whether establishing new workflows, developing applications, or as part of ongoing improvement, take advantage of the recommendations provided by Trusted Advisor on a regular basis to help keep your solutions provisioned optimally.

_Note_: [AWS Trusted Advisor](https://aws.amazon.com/premiumsupport/technology/trusted-advisor/)  provides an aggregated view for AWS accounts and sub accounts in an [AWS Organization](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/organizations/latest/userguide/orgs_introduction.html)  only for the billing-related parts. For Technical information (such as low utilized Amazon EC2 instances, underutilized Amazon EBS Volumes, etc.) you have to go into each related accounts. Said otherwise, if you are in the [root account](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/organizations/latest/userguide/orgs_getting-started_concepts.html)  of an organization and the "Underutilized Amazon EBS Volumes" check is green, and you don't have any suggestion, it doesn't mean that you don't have "Underutilized Amazon EBS Volumes." You have different solutions for that for the simplest to the most complete:

*   Leverage ["Organizational view" reports](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/awssupport/latest/user/organizational-view.html) ; more details in the [View AWS Trusted Advisor recommendations at scale with AWS Organizations](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/mt/organizational-view-for-trusted-advisor/)  blog post.
*   Follow the [Enterprise Dashboard lab](https://www.wellarchitectedlabs.com/cost/200_labs/200_enterprise_dashboards/) ; it will create a global dashboard that you can share among your organization.

AWS Trusted Advisor won't provide the same features depending on your [support plan](https://aws.amazon.com/premiumsupport/plans/) . For the most up-to-date information, please visit the [AWS Trusted Advisor FAQ](https://aws.amazon.com/premiumsupport/faqs/) .
