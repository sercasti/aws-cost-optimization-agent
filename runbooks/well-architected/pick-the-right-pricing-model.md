# Pick the Right Pricing Model

The following chart is a ten thousand feet overview that provides a rule of thumbs to determine the right pricing model to choose. You can combine different purchase options to fit your requirements.

![Combine purchase options](https://static.us-east-1.prod.workshops.aws/public/8844589c-f6be-4752-bd27-0dad323d1eb9/static/images/001_intro/002_core_activities/purchase-options.png?classes=shadow&width=500px)

*   Use **RIs and Savings Plans** for known, steady-state workloads,
*   **On-Demand** for unknown spiky workloads,
*   Scale using **Spot** for any fault-tolerant, flexible and stateless workloads.

On-demand or [Pay-as-you-go](https://aws.amazon.com/pricing/)  pricing allows you to easily adapt to changing business needs and improving your responsiveness to changes. You do not need to forecast. Pay-as-you-go pricing model reduces the risk of over-provisioning or missing capacity. That is the default pricing model; among others: Amazon EC2 Instances, Amazon Relational Databases Service, Amazon Redshift, Amazon Elasticsearch Service, Amazon Elasticache.

[Savings Plans](https://aws.amazon.com/savingsplans/)  is billing discount that allows you to save on your AWS compute usage. This pricing model offers lower prices on Amazon EC2 instances usage, regardless of instance family, size, OS, tenancy or AWS Region, and also applies to AWS Fargate and AWS Lambda usage. You commit to use a specific amount of compute power (measured in $/hour).

An Amazon Reserved Instances (RI) is a billing discount that allows you to save on your Amazon EC2 usage costs. When you purchase a Reserved Instance, you can set attributes such as instance or node type, platform, tenancy, Region, or Availability Zone (optional).

Amazon Spot let you take advantage of unused capacity in the AWS cloud. For instance [Amazon EC2 Spot Instances](https://aws.amazon.com/ec2/spot/)  are available at up to a 90% discount compared to On-Demand prices.

#### Spot

](#spot)

Choose Spot for various workloads that are stateless, fault-tolerant, and loosely-coupled or flexible applications such as big data, containerized workloads, CI/CD, web servers, high-performance computing (HPC), and test & development workloads. Spot applies on EC2, or managed services such as EMR, Elastic Beanstalk, SageMaker, ECS, EKS, and Fargate.

#### AWS Compute Savings Plans

](#aws-compute-savings-plans)

[AWS Compute Savings Plans](https://aws.amazon.com/savingsplans/pricing/)  provides the most flexibility across:

*   **Instance family**: ex. Move from C5 to M5
*   **Region**: ex. Change from EU (Ireland) to EU (London)
*   **OS**: ex. Windows to Linux
*   **Tenancy**: ex Switch from Dedicated tenancy to Default tenancy
*   **Compute options**: ex. Move from EC2 to AWS Fargate or AWS Lambda

Implementation time

Savings potential

Time to realize savings

Commitment required

Hours

Up to 49% (EC2), 20% (Fargate), 12% (Lambda) cheaper than On-Demand

Hours

1 year, No Upfront costs

[Savings](https://aws.amazon.com/savingsplans/pricing/)  can go up to **72%** for Amazon EC2, **52%** for AWS Fargate and **17%** for AWS Lambda for 3 years, all upfront commitments. Please visit pricing pages for up to date information.

1.  Review your Savings Plans [recommendations](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/savingsplans/latest/userguide/get-started.html)  in AWS Cost Explorer,
2.  Customize recommendations based on your needs (Term Length: 1 year, Payment Option: No Upfront),
3.  Add preferred Savings Plans amount to cart and purchase.
