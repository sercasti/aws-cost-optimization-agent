# Savings Plans

[Savings Plans](https://aws.amazon.com/savingsplans/)  is a flexible pricing model that provides savings of up to 72% on your AWS compute usage. This pricing model offers lower prices on Amazon EC2 instances usage, regardless of instance family, size, OS, tenancy or AWS Region, and also applies to AWS Fargate and AWS Lambda usage.

Savings Plans offer significant savings over On Demand, just like EC2 Reserved Instances, in exchange for a commitment to use a specific amount of compute power (measured in $/hour) for a one or three year period. You can sign up for Savings Plans for a 1- or 3-year term and easily manage your plans by taking advantage of recommendations, performance reporting and budget alerts in the AWS Cost Explorer.

Once you may have purchased Savings Plans here a quick reference guide to understand the Savings Plans reports.

[

#### Tip #1: Using the utilization report

](#tip-1:-using-the-utilization-report)

Savings Plans utilization shows you the percentage of your Savings Plans commitment that you're using across your compute usage.

You can access it from [AWS Cost Management](https://console.aws.amazon.com/cost-management/home?region=us-east-1#/savings-plans/utilization) : Cost Explorer > Savings Plans > Utilization report

![Utilization report](https://static.us-east-1.prod.workshops.aws/public/8844589c-f6be-4752-bd27-0dad323d1eb9/static/images/003_tools/006_savings_plans/sp_utilization_report.png?classes=shadow)

[

#### Tip #2: Using the coverage report

](#tip-2:-using-the-coverage-report)

The Savings Plans coverage report shows how much of your eligible spend was covered by your Savings Plans, based on the selected time period.

You can access it from [AWS Cost Management](https://console.aws.amazon.com/cost-management/home?region=us-east-1#/savings-plans/coverage) : Cost Explorer > Savings Plans > Coverage report

![Coverage report graph](https://static.us-east-1.prod.workshops.aws/public/8844589c-f6be-4752-bd27-0dad323d1eb9/static/images/003_tools/006_savings_plans/sp_coverage_report.png?classes=shadow)

The column "_**Spend covered by Savings Plan**_" represents the On-demand equivalent. The column "_**On-demand spend**_" represents the On-demand spent not covered by Savings Plan.
