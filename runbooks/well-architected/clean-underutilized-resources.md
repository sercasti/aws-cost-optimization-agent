# Clean Underutilized Resources

> "The best energy is the one that is not consumed, the problem is that we use too much"

Your current AWS usage might be really different from your usage when you started to use AWS services. You may have created RDS, Redshift, Route 53, ELBs, EIPs, and EBS resources that are now underutilized or even not used at all. Cleaning unused resources can represent a large amount of savings.

Your preferred solution here is AWS Trusted Advisor.
